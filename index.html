How it works

Three steps to your own app launcher

No accounts. No configuration files. Just open, add, and go.

01

Add a website URL

Paste any link — a productivity tool, a news site, your favorite game. Fab Hub fetches the favicon, cleans the title, and picks a brand color automatically.

02

Fab Hub finds the icon and name

No manual work. The favicon becomes your app icon. The page title gets stripped of "Home |", "Official Site", "— Wikipedia", and similar clutter. You can edit anything before saving.

03

Launch it fullscreen

Tap to open in a chromeless viewer with a floating handle for back, reload, and tabs. Add to home screen for a truly native feel. Works offline with custom fallback pages.
