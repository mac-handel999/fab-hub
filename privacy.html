Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026. Last updated: November 27, 2026.

1. No tracking, no accounts, no nonsense

Fab Hub does not collect personal data. No analytics, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no third-party trackers. Your app list, settings, and preferences stay in your browser's localStorage — on your device, under your control.

2. What stays local

  • Your apps: URLs, custom names, icons, brand colors, and per-app settings (fullscreen, dark mode, wake lock, lock).
  • Recents & trash: Recently opened apps and deleted items (auto-purged after 30 days).
  • Session state: Open tabs and scroll positions for session restore.
  • Theme preference: System / Light / Dark choice.
  • Favicon cache: Cached favicon URLs to avoid repeated fetches.

All of the above is stored in localStorage under keys prefixed with fabhub.. You can clear it anytime from Settings → Clear data.

3. Network requests

Fab Hub makes only these network requests:

  • Favicon fetching: When you add a URL, our /api/icon endpoint fetches the site's favicon server-side (with SSRF protection). The favicon URL is cached locally.
  • Site metadata: /api/meta fetches the page title and theme color for the "Add app" flow.
  • Embed check: /api/frameable checks X-Frame-Options and Content-Security-Policy to know if a site can be embedded.
  • Search (optional): If you set a BRAVE_API_KEY in Vercel env vars, /api/search returns live web results. Without it, search falls back to the local catalog.json.
  • PWA manifest & service worker: Standard files for installability and offline support.

No request sends your app list, browsing history, or identifiers to our servers.

4. Third-party content

When you open an app, its content loads in an iframe from the original domain. That site's own privacy policy applies. Fab Hub does not proxy, modify, or inspect the framed content.

5. Share target

If you share a URL to Fab Hub (via Web Share Target), the URL is processed locally to add the app. It is not sent to any server.

6. Data retention & deletion

  • Trash items are auto-deleted after 30 days.
  • Favicon cache entries have no hard expiry but can be cleared via Settings → Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the PWA clears all localStorage and service worker caches.

7. Children's privacy

Fab Hub is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
